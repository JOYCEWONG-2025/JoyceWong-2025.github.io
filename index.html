AI AGENT · UM HACKATHON 2026

Role

Built independently at UM Hackathon 2026.

A human-in-the-loop document workflow engine. It redacts personally identifiable information first, reasons over the redacted text with an LLM agent, screens the output against policy rules, then pauses for a human to approve before anything executes — with a hash-chained audit log for every decision.

FastAPI, LangGraph, React + TypeScript
